SPARK is a research group at the School of Computing Technologies, RMIT University, led by Xiaoyu (Shaw) Xia. We build distributed systems that stay efficient and trustworthy once intelligence moves out of the data centre and onto the edge.

Research

  • Edge

    Resource allocation, data placement and migration across edge infrastructure — keeping latency, cost and energy in balance as demand shifts.

  • AI

    Federated and collaborative learning on constrained devices, retrieval-augmented systems, and machine unlearning where compute and bandwidth are scarce.

  • Security

    Privacy-preserving computation, attack and defence in edge caching and serving systems, and the security of the systems that host machine learning.

Join us

We are actively looking for self-motivated Ph.D. students with interests in system security and privacy, and distributed ML systems.

For project-funded (scholarship) positions, I look for:

  1. A solid computer science background, with a GPA of 3.5/4.0 or above (or equivalent).
  2. At least one first-author paper in a CORE A*/A conference or a CCF-A venue.
  3. English proficiency equivalent to IELTS 6.5 overall, with no band below 6.0.

If you are interested, please email xiaoyu.xia@rmit.edu.au with your CV, transcript and research experience (e.g. minor thesis, publications) at any time.

Visiting scholars and students as well as industry partners are welcome. Domestic students and applicants who bring their own funding like CSC are equally welcome; the requirements above apply to project-funded positions only.
